Noa is the secretary of the student council "Seminar" at Millennium Science School. She has a very good memory and can memorize almost everything she sees or hears once, including decisions made at the "Seminar" and records of the contents of meetings. 

Noa is a helpful member of Seminar. Utilizing her incredible memory, she is able to record nearly everything that's been said during meetings, as well as details of locations she has been to that others may overlook entirely. This leaves her as a capable investigator in addition to an observer. She often poses a calming front and often has a subtle smile that she holds for long periods with ease. Noa has been friends with Yuuka for some time and periodically teases her, especially regarding her feelings towards Sensei. However, she is also the only member of Seminar capable of intimidating Koyuki into cooperation or compliance.

Her hobbies include poetry, though she often uses complex or archaic terminology in her writing. She lives as an ascetic, believing that a minimalist lifestyle contributes to her ability to be an impartial observer. 

She has ankle-length white hair cut into blunt bangs. Her eyes are a bright purple. Noa's figure is soft and pronounced. A pair of mechanical devices resembling Scouter lenses sit upon her ears, though their purpose is unknown. 

Noa's uniform consists of white and blue low heel boots, black tights, a pleated white skirt and blazer with a tech adorned jacket hung over her arms. Underneath the blazer is a white dress-shirt and blue tie with her Millennium access card clipped onto the blazer's side pocket.

Gym version: She wore a light blue uniform jacket zipped down to the chest, a dark blue T-shirt underneath, and gym buruma shorts with two white stripes on the hips sides. On her feet are white sneakers with black soles. 

Noa's voice actor is none other than Suzushiro Sayumi, who voiced U-101 and later Napoli in "Azur Lane" and Ichiji Nijika in "Bocchi The Rock!".
Noa shares a strong friendship with Yuuka, as seen in the "Sensei, May I Have A Moment of Your Time?" shorts. Yuuka and Noa frequently appear together in video shorts and official artwork.
Noa was first mentioned in Volume 2 Chapter 1; in the episodes, she might be having fun with one of the gates during the fight between C&C and the Engineering Department.
As mentioned above, her normal debut is from On Your Mark ＠ Millennium before the events of Volume 2 Chapter 2 takes place.
Noa's voice is teased on "Sensei, May I Have A Moment of Your Time?" in Episode 12, when Yuuka is typing. Her 3D debut also appears in Episode 13 and onwards.
In Episode 22, when Noa makes her debut in the series, she says, 'ごめんえ、ユウカちゃん～' ('I'm sorry, Yuuka-chan~'). This line quickly sparked a resurgence of the 'Gomen Yuuka' memes, which trended online and playfully labeled Noa as a 'cheater' or associated her with netorare. It's also confirmed that Yostar/Nexon itself is embracing all of the memes the community created, and have added it as a nod to Noa's reputation as a potent seductress.

    Of additional note is her presence in a fan-made club known as the "Dryness Squad", consisting of herself, Urawa Hanako, Wanibuchi Akari, and Izayoi Nonomi, who all bear similar figures and reputations as having exceptional desire for Sensei and the stamina to match.

Her Memorial Lobby features a French line that reads "Qui aimes-tu le mieux, homme énigmatique, dis ?", which translates to "Whom do you love the best, enigmatic man?".

In the 1st PV, Noa is shown reading a book called Flowers of Evil (惡の華), which is a reference to Charles Baudelaire's most famous work: a poetry collection of nearly all his works, titled Les Fleurs du mal (The Flowers of Evil).
Her gun, Secretary's Vote, is the third project involving Tokyo Marui hence why their company name is imprinted on the gun.
The Raider Brace seems to be Tokyo Marui's own design since the Raider design are not intended for 1911 or 2011 style handguns but for full-sized SIG Sauer P320 pistols only. Besides being a pistol, her weapon is a "SMG" for weapon reasons.
The camera angle during Noa's EX Skill CGI cutscene where she's reloading her Secretary's Vote pistol refers to the 20th to 24th second of the Flux Raider ad.[1]
Earlier concept arts present Noa wielding a railgun instead of a handgun, possibly turning her into the first playable wielder of such a weapon if not for Alice. This possibly implies that she was originally intended to fill the role that Alice did in Vol. 2 and Vol. F ("Noa's Ark"), but that this was changed for unknown reasons.

    The design of Noa's original railgun would later see minor changes and be used by Kei, likely as a nod to the initial concept for her.

Unlike her Seminar companions, Noa has a trait of being particularly nonathletic, likely due to her secretary role. This was evidenced during Chapter 198 of JP's 4-Koma and 2025's New Years greetings, where she was grasping for air after arriving to the SCHALE office, compared to a fully energetic Koyuki who rushed in an out of the building with ease.

Hayase Yuuka

A fellow member of Seminar who works as Millennium's treasurer. Since Yuuka is always overworking herself, Noa tends to help her out whenever she can. The two have been close friends for some time and little seems to threaten it.
